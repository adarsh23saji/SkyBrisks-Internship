EpigraphiX-AI

A neural palm-leaf manuscript OCR and epigraphical intelligence suite designed for degraded historical documents.

Highlights

  • Combines computer vision preprocessing with neural OCR for difficult palm-leaf manuscript imagery.
  • Uses TrOCR, CNNs and classical machine-learning models for transcription and classification workflows.
  • Includes NLP components such as Levenshtein alignment and trie-based lexicon processing.
  • Generates structured document-intelligence outputs and vector PDF reports.
